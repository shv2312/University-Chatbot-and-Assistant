CAMPUS RESIDENCE & HOSTEL GUIDELINES MANUAL
Office of Student Housing and Residential Life | Academic Year 2025-2026

SECTION 1: CURFEW AND ENTRY/EXIT PROTOCOLS
1.1 Daily Curfew Hours:
The hostel gate curfew is strictly 8:30 PM on all weekdays and weekends. All residents must biometric scan at the main residence gate before 8:30 PM. Late entry without prior written sanction from the Chief Warden will attract disciplinary fines and parental notification.

1.2 Night Out-Pass Policy:
Residents wishing to leave campus overnight must submit a formal digital out-pass through the Student Portal at least 24 hours in advance, countersigned by their verified guardian or parent.

SECTION 2: VISITOR POLICY
2.1 Permitted Visiting Hours:
Outside visitors, including parents and guardians, are permitted ONLY between 4:30 PM and 6:30 PM on designated days.
2.2 Permitted Areas:
All visitor interactions must occur exclusively in the designated ground floor Reception and Visitor Lounge. Under no circumstances are visitors permitted entry into student living quarters or dormitory corridors.

SECTION 3: DINING HALL AND MESS TIMINGS
Residents must strictly observe the following mess service timings:
- Breakfast: 7:30 AM to 8:45 AM
- Lunch: 12:30 PM to 1:45 PM
- Evening Snacks & Tea: 4:45 PM to 5:30 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM
Food service counters close promptly at the end of each slot. Taking dining crockery, utensils, or food containers into private rooms is strictly prohibited.

SECTION 4: SILENT STUDY HOURS & PROHIBITED APPLIANCES
4.1 Silent Study Hours:
Silent study hours commence daily at 9:30 PM and extend until 6:00 AM. Loud music, group gatherings in corridors, and disturbances during these hours are strictly forbidden.

4.2 Electrical Equipment Restrictions:
High-wattage appliances such as immersion heaters, electric stoves, induction cooktops, and heavy heaters are strictly banned in student rooms due to fire safety regulations.
