UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & CURRICULUM POLICY MANUAL
Academic Year: 2025-2026 | Office of the Dean of Academic Affairs

SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS AND CONDONATION
1.1 Minimum Mandatory Attendance:
Every registered student is required to secure a minimum aggregate attendance of 75% in each registered theory, tutorial, and laboratory course to be eligible to appear for the End Semester Examinations.

1.2 Condonation for Medical or Extenuating Reasons:
Students securing attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for attendance condonation subject to the following criteria:
- Production of valid, certified medical proof from a registered medical practitioner within 7 working days of recovery.
- Official approval by the Head of Department (HoD) and Dean of Student Affairs.
- Payment of the prescribed condonation processing fee of Rs. 1,000 per subject.
Students with attendance strictly below 65% are categorically not eligible to appear for the semester examinations under any circumstances and must repeat the course during subsequent semesters.

SECTION 2: EVALUATION AND GRADING SYSTEM
2.1 Assessment Breakdown:
Course performance is evaluated through Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) carrying 40% weightage, and the End Semester Examination (ESE) carrying 60% weightage.

2.2 Passing Criteria:
To pass a registered course, a student must secure:
- A minimum of 45% marks in the End Semester Examination (27 out of 60 marks).
- An aggregate total of at least 50 marks (out of 100 marks) combining Internal Assessment and the End Semester Examination.

2.3 Grading Scale and Grade Points:
Letter grades and corresponding grade points are awarded as follows:
- Grade O (Outstanding): 90 to 100 marks (Grade Point: 10)
- Grade A+ (Excellent): 80 to 89 marks (Grade Point: 9)
- Grade A (Very Good): 70 to 79 marks (Grade Point: 8)
- Grade B+ (Good): 60 to 69 marks (Grade Point: 7)
- Grade B (Above Average): 55 to 59 marks (Grade Point: 6)
- Grade C (Average / Minimum Pass): 50 to 54 marks (Grade Point: 5)
- Grade RA (Re-Appearance / Fail): Less than 50 marks (Grade Point: 0)

SECTION 3: SUPPLEMENTARY EXAMINATIONS & REVALUATION
Students obtaining an RA grade must register for the supplementary examination in the subsequent examination cycle. Applications for answer script photocopy and revaluation must be submitted within 10 working days following result publication.
