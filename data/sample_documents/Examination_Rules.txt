OFFICE OF THE CONTROLLER OF EXAMINATIONS
CODE OF CONDUCT & EXAMINATION HALL REGULATIONS | Academic Year 2025-2026

SECTION 1: REPORTING TIME AND ENTRY PROTOCOLS
1.1 Candidate Seating Schedule:
All candidates must report to their assigned examination hall and be seated at least 15 minutes before the scheduled commencement of the examination. The exam bell rings 5 minutes prior to the start for distribution of answer booklets.

1.2 Late Entry Grace Period:
No candidate shall be permitted to enter the examination hall after 30 minutes have elapsed from the commencement of the examination. No extra time will be granted for late arrivals.

SECTION 2: MANDATORY IDENTIFICATION & CREDENTIALS
2.1 Required Documents:
Every student must present an original, valid physical Examination Hall Ticket and the Official University Student Photo ID Card to the hall invigilator. Students lacking either credential will not be permitted to take the examination unless an emergency clearance certificate is issued by the Chief Superintendent.

SECTION 3: STRICTLY PROHIBITED ITEMS AND ELECTRONIC DEVICES
3.1 Banned Electronic Items:
Mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, Bluetooth earphones, fitness bands, recording devices, and any unauthorized digital hardware are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall.
Possession of any prohibited device—whether powered on, in airplane mode, or powered off—is considered an act of examination malpractice and results in immediate confiscation and cancellation of the paper.

SECTION 4: EXAMINATION HALL DISCIPLINE AND LEAVING THE HALL
4.1 Permissible Stationery:
Candidates may bring only transparent pencil pouches containing approved writing instruments (pens, pencils, erasers, non-programmable scientific calculators where specified on the question paper). Borrowing stationery between candidates is strictly forbidden.

4.2 Early Departure Guidelines:
No candidate is permitted to submit their answer booklet or permanently leave the examination hall during the first 60 minutes or during the final 10 minutes of the examination duration.
